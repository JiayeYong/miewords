## GRE Vocabulary 3000

The built-in `GRE（镇考 3000 词）` list is adapted from
[`0xDkXy/GRE_vocabulary_3000`](https://github.com/0xDkXy/GRE_vocabulary_3000),
retrieved from its `master` branch. MieWords retains the English headwords and
extracts concise Chinese translations from `GRE3000.csv`; phonetics, example
sentences, list order, and other columns are omitted.
