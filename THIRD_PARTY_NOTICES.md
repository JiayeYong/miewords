# Third-party notices

## GRE Vocabulary 3000

The built-in `GRE（镇考 3000 词）` list is adapted from
[`0xDkXy/GRE_vocabulary_3000`](https://github.com/0xDkXy/GRE_vocabulary_3000),
retrieved from its `master` branch. MieWords retains the English headwords and
extracts concise Chinese translations from `GRE3000.csv`; phonetics, example
sentences, list order, and other columns are omitted.

The source repository is published under the MIT License. A copy is included
at [`licenses/GRE_VOCABULARY_3000_LICENSE.txt`](licenses/GRE_VOCABULARY_3000_LICENSE.txt).

## ECDICT TOEFL vocabulary

The built-in `TOEFL（ECDICT 词库）` list is extracted from entries carrying the
`toefl` tag in [`skywind3000/ECDICT`](https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT).
MieWords retains only the English headword and a cleaned Chinese translation;
phonetics, parts of speech, English definitions, frequency metadata, and other
dictionary fields are omitted.

ECDICT is published under the MIT License. A copy is included at
[`licenses/ECDICT_LICENSE.txt`](licenses/ECDICT_LICENSE.txt).
